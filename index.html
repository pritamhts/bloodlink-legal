BloodLink Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

BloodLink helps people find blood donors, submit blood requests, and discover donor organizations and ambulance services.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, BloodLink may process your name, phone number, email address, blood group, district, upazian profile photo, donor status, blood requests, donor responses, organization or ambulance service details, notifications, device push tokens, and location information you choose to provide.

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate accounts, display donor and service listings, connect people who need blood with potential donors, manage requests and responses, deliver notifications, protect the service, and support account deletion.

Service Providers and Sharing

BloodLink uses Supabase for authentication, database and storage services. Information may be made visible to other authenticated users where necessary for donor discovery and blood-request functionality. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information in public-facing listings.

Location and Photos

Location information and profile photos may be processed when you use relevant features. Avoid sharing information you do not want other users to see.

Retention and Account Deletion

You can use the in-app Delete Account option to request deletion of your account and associated data. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or technical logs where applicable, or be retained when legally required. Data connected to other users' activity may require separate handling.

Security

We use technical measures intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

BloodLink is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact
