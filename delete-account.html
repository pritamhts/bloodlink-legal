Delete Your BloodLink Account

This page explains how BloodLink users can request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

Option 1: Delete from the app

  1. Open BloodLink and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile screen.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning and select Delete permanently to confirm.

Option 2: If you cannot access the app

If you cannot sign in or use the in-app deletion option, use the developer's published contact method to request account deletion. The developer must publish a working contact method here before this page is considered complete.

What information is deleted?

Deletion is intended to remove your authentication account and associated profile, donor information, blood requests, responses submitted by you, notifications, push tokens, saved location data, profile photo, and organization or ambulance-service listings owned by you.

Important: Deletion may be irreversible. Information related to other users' activity, technical logs, or backups may require separate handling. Do not assume every copy is removed immediately unless the developer has verified it.
